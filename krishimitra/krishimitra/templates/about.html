About Krishi Mitra

AI-Based Farmer Query Support & Decision Assistance System

Project Overview

Krishi Mitra is an integrated artificial intelligence platform designed to address critical challenges in Indian agriculture. By combining Convolutional Neural Networks (CNN) for leaf disease diagnosis, real-time government market price APIs, live meteorological advisories, and soil NPK fertilizer calculators, Krishi Mitra provides actionable, localized support directly to farmers.

The primary objective is accessibility—ensuring that complex AI models and agricultural data are presented through simple, multilingual, and voice-assisted web interfaces suitable for rural users.

🛠️ Technology Stack
  • Backend Framework: Django 5.2 (Python)
  • Machine Learning: TensorFlow 2.21, Keras, OpenCV, NumPy
  • Database: SQLite 3 (ORM with Django Models)
  • External APIs: Agmarknet API (GOI OGD), Open-Meteo Weather API
  • Frontend & UX: HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, Bootstrap 5.3, AOS Animations
  • Voice Synthesis & STT: Web Speech API (SpeechRecognition & SpeechSynthesis)
🎯 Our Mission

Empower Indian farmers with accessible, timely, and scientific agricultural guidance to reduce crop loss, optimize fertilizer usage, and maximize crop yields.

👁️ Our Vision

To bridge the technological gap between modern AI advancements and rural agricultural practices through intuitive, multi-modal digital solutions.
