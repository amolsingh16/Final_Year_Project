About Krishi Mitra & Project Team

AI-Based Farmer Query Support & Decision Assistance System

🌿 Project Overview

Krishi Mitra is an integrated Artificial Intelligence platform designed to address critical challenges in Indian agriculture. By combining Convolutional Neural Networks (CNN) and Supervised U-Net segmentation for leaf disease diagnosis, real-time Agmarknet 2.0 market price APIs, Open-Meteo meteorological advisories, and ICAR soil NPK fertilizer calculators, Krishi Mitra provides actionable, localized support directly to farmers.

Our primary goal is accessibility — ensuring that deep learning models and real-time government datasets are presented through an intuitive, bilingual (Hindi & English), and voice-assisted interface suitable for every farmer.

🛠️ Technology Stack

Backend Framework Django 5.2 (Python 3)
Deep Learning & Vision TensorFlow 2.21, Keras, OpenCV
Live Market & Weather APIs Agmarknet 2.0 & Open-Meteo
Voice & Frontend UX Web Speech API, Bootstrap 5.3
🎯 Our Mission

Empower Indian farmers with accessible, timely, and scientific agricultural guidance to reduce crop loss, optimize fertilizer usage, and maximize crop yields.

👁️ Our Vision

To bridge the technological gap between modern AI advancements and rural agricultural practices through intuitive, multi-modal digital solutions.
