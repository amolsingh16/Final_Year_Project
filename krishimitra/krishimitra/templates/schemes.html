Government Agriculture Schemes Directory

Explore official welfare schemes, credit cards, insurance, and financial benefits for Indian farmers.

ℹ️ Note: Krishi Mitra provides informational guidance. Click Apply via Official Portal to visit the official Government of India websites directly.
Direct Income Support Ministry of Agri
PM-KISAN Samman Nidhi

Financial benefit of ₹6,000 per year in three equal installments transferred directly into bank accounts of landholding farmer families.

  • Eligibility: Small & Marginal landholder farmers
  • Required: Aadhaar card, Bank account, Land records
Apply via Official Portal ↗
Crop Insurance Risk Coverage
PM Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY)

Comprehensive crop insurance scheme covering yield losses due to non-preventable natural risks (drought, flood, pests, disease).

  • Premium: 2% for Kharif, 1.5% for Rabi crops
  • Coverage: Sowing to post-harvest risks
Apply via Official Portal ↗
Agri Credit Low Interest
Kisan Credit Card (KCC)

Provides adequate and timely credit to farmers for crop cultivation, post-harvest expenses, and allied agricultural activities at subsidized 4% interest rate.

  • Limit: Up to ₹3 Lakh collateral-free loan
  • Scope: Crops, Animal Husbandry & Fisheries
Apply via Official Portal ↗
Soil Quality Testing
Soil Health Card Scheme

Provides soil nutrient status report (12 parameters) to farmers along with recommended fertilizer doses to improve crop productivity.

  • Frequency: Issued once every 2 years
  • Benefit: Reduces excess fertilizer costs
Official Portal ↗
Micro-Irrigation Water Efficiency
PM Krishi Sinchayee Yojana

Enhance physical access to water on farm and expand cultivable area under assured micro-irrigation ('Per Drop More Crop').

  • Subsidy: 45% - 55% for Drip/Sprinkler sets
  • Focus: Water conservation and precision farming
Official Portal ↗
Machinery Subsidy Equipment
Agri Machinery Subsidy (SMAM)

Financial assistance for purchasing agricultural equipment (Tractors, Harvesters, Rotavators, Seed drills) and setting up Custom Hiring Centers.

  • Subsidy: 40% - 80% based on farmer category
  • Target: Small & marginal farmers
