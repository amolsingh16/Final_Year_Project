💰 ₹6,000 / Year
PM-KISAN Samman Nidhi

पीएम किसान सम्मान निधि योजना

Direct income support of ₹6,000 per year in three equal installments (₹2,000 every 4 months) transferred via DBT into farmers' bank accounts.

  • Eligibility: All landholding farmer families
  • Documents: Aadhaar Card, Land Khasra/Khatauni, Bank Passbook

🛡️ Crop Insurance
PM Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY)

प्रधानमंत्री फसल बीमा योजना

Comprehensive crop insurance against natural calamities, drought, flood, unseasonal rainfall, pests, and plant diseases.

  • Farmer Premium: 2% Kharif, 1.5% Rabi, 5% Commercial
  • Claim Helpline: 14447 (72-hour loss intimation)
Apply via Official Portal ↗
💳 4% Interest Loan
Kisan Credit Card (KCC)

किसान क्रेडिट कार्ड योजना

Timely, low-interest credit for seeds, fertilizers, pesticides, and allied activities (dairy, fisheries) at an effective 4% p.a. rate.

  • Collateral-Free: Up to ₹1.60 Lakh – ₹3 Lakh limit
  • Scope: Crops, Dairy, Poultry & Fisheries
Apply via Official Portal ↗
☀️ 60% Solar Subsidy
PM-KUSUM Solar Pump Scheme

पीएम कुसुम सोलर पंप सब्सिडी

Install standalone solar agricultural water pumps (3 HP to 10 HP) with up to 60% Central + State government subsidy and zero diesel cost.

  • Subsidy: 60% Govt Grant + 30% Bank Loan
  • Farmer Share: Only 10% upfront cost
Official Portal ↗
🧪 12 Soil Parameters
Soil Health Card Scheme

मृदा स्वास्थ्य कार्ड योजना

Provides scientific testing of 12 soil parameters (N, P, K, pH, EC, OC, S, Zn, Fe, Cu, Mn, B) with crop-wise fertilizer recommendations.

  • Cycle: Renewed every 2 years per farm plot
  • Benefit: Saves 20–25% on chemical fertilizer cost
Official Portal ↗
💧 Per Drop More Crop
PM Krishi Sinchayee Yojana

प्रधानमंत्री कृषि सिंचाई योजना

Financial subsidy for installing Drip and Sprinkler micro-irrigation systems to conserve water and boost yield.

  • Subsidy: 55% for Small/Marginal, 45% for Others
  • Water Saving: Up to 50% water & fertilizer efficiency
Official Portal ↗
🚜 40%–80% Subsidy
Agri Machinery Subsidy (SMAM)

कृषि यंत्र सब्सिडी योजना

Grant support for buying Rotavators, Seed Drills, Power Tillers, Drones, Harvesters, and setting up Custom Hiring Centers (CHC).

  • Individual Subsidy: 40% to 50% on farm machines
  • FPO / CHC: Up to 80% subsidy for group banks
Official Portal ↗
📈 1,389+ Mandis
e-NAM Digital Mandi Portal

राष्ट्रीय कृषि बाज़ार (ई-नाम)

Pan-India electronic trading portal connecting APMC mandis for transparent online bidding and direct bank payment to farmers.

  • Toll-Free Help: 1800-270-0224
  • Benefit: Better price discovery without middlemen
